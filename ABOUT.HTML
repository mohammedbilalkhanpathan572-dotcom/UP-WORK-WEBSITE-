What is Upwork Business Plus and how do I join?

Upwork Business Plus is a membership plan that gives clients premium hiring tools, recruiting, and support to manage freelancer contracts more effectively. It is billed on a 30-day cycle with no upfront costs and you can get started online.

Read more
What benefits do I get with Upwork Business Plus?

In addition to all the features and benefits available with Upwork's Basic plan, Business Plus includes premium talent matching, advanced hiring tools and priority support.

Read more
How much does Upwork Business Plus cost?

Business Plus is free to join and runs on a 30-day membership cycle. When you hire on Upwork, you pay the applicable fees shown during checkout and in your membership terms.

Read more
How do I cancel Business Plus?

Any client with Account Admin access can request to downgrade their Business Plus plan to the Basic plan. Review the current membership terms for details.

Read more
How can I upgrade to Upwork Business Plus?

Eligible clients can upgrade their Basic plan to Business Plus using their account's membership settings.

Read more
How do I get 30-day payment terms?

Eligible Business Plus clients may apply for monthly invoicing with 30-day payment terms through approved third-party partners. Approval requirements and eligibility conditions may apply.
